AI Predictive Analysis

Prediction Engine · Linear Regression
The AI fits a trend line through the last 20 sensor readings (40 seconds of data). It then extends that line forward to predict when gas levels will reach dangerous thresholds. If gas is rising, it tells you how many seconds or minutes you have before action is needed.
📈 MQ-2 trend Is combustible gas rising, falling or stable? -
📈 MQ-135 trend Is air-quality gas rising, falling or stable? -

🔴 ETA to danger Estimated time until gas hits the 🔴 danger level (if currently rising) -
🎯 Risk score 0 = no risk · 100 = critical. Combines current level, rate of rise & anomalies -
MQ-2 slope How fast the combustible-gas level is changing per 2-second reading -
MQ-2 R² (confidence) How well the trend line fits the data. 1.0 = perfect fit; near 0 = erratic/no clear trend -
Anomaly Detection · Z-Score
The AI monitors what is "normal" for this environment (last 20 readings). If a new reading is unusually far from normal (Z > 2.5), it flags an ANOMALY — a sudden unexpected spike that slow threshold alarms would miss entirely.
MQ-2 anomaly NORMAL = within expected range · ANOMALY = sudden unusual spike detected NORMAL
MQ-135 anomaly NORMAL = within expected range · ANOMALY = sudden unusual spike detected NORMAL
MQ-2 Z-score How many "standard deviations" the latest reading is from the recent average. >2.5 = anomaly -
MQ-135 Z-score Same measure for air-quality sensor. A sudden gas burst registers here first -
MQ-2 mean (window) Average combustible-gas reading over the last 20 readings — the "normal baseline" -
MQ-2 std dev (σ) How much the readings typically wobble up/down. Small = steady; large = variable environment -
How to read this: Z > 2.5 flags an anomaly · Window = last 20 readings (40 s) · Updates every 2 s
